# PR08 — Rotation and The Stubborn Gate

## Level 2
Choose Level 2 directly from the title (Level 1 and practice remain separately accessible). The floor is at y=3; the gate spans y=3 to 8 with a fixed overhead hinge H=(14,8). The lintel and bracket are solid geometry. The gate is a genuine obstruction, not a locked completion flag. It is taller than a normal jump, and the lintel prevents jumping over it. The player must select the gate, choose Rotation, enter the angle, direction and exact centre, preview the signed arc, and apply if the path and endpoint are clear. One intended move is 90 degrees anticlockwise about H. The gate then lies at y=8 to 8.5 and the apprentice can walk underneath. A clockwise quarter-turn cuts into the floor; the equivalent 270-degree clockwise endpoint would pass outside the room. The latter is deliberately not accepted just because its final coordinates are the same. Opposite direction can safely close the already-open gate, so corrective casts are real moves. Hints reveal the mechanic, compare coordinates and then give a worked description. No answer equality check at exit.

## Rotation mathematics and safety
- PR06's exact quarter-turn formulas establish the final geometry and stable reference vertices. The fixed hinge must match the specified centre exactly for commit. Other rotate-capable supported/enchanted objects can use external centres. Angle choices: 90, 180 or 270 degrees; either direction. All mathematics uses y-up world coordinates.
- Compute a signed angular interval. For every moving vertex and fixed edge line, solve A cos(t)+B sin(t)=C. Also test fixed vertices against moving edges by inverse rotation. The sorted roots (with tolerance-offset lines) partition the sweep into intervals with no vertex/edge crossing; test endpoints/events and interval interiors for positive-area overlap using the simple-polygon validator. This is an event-based check, not fixed-step angular sampling. Check every vertex's coordinate extrema during the arc against room bounds. As in PR07, check solids, hazards, player and final support, and reject moving an object supporting the player. Pure touching is allowed. Floating-point tolerances apply; this is not a general rigid-body or arbitrary continuous physics engine.
- The inspector shows original/image vertices, centre, rays, signed arc arrow and angle/direction. Gate material and straps turn with the polygon. A clear final pose alone cannot enable Apply.
- Apply revalidates current state and matching parameters; translation and rotation use the same internal atomic transaction to return immutable new room geometry and a matching collision index. A move counts only after a successful commit. Rotation animates around the actual centre for 0.75s, with simulation and competing spell input paused; reduced-motion skips animation. Reset restores the original room, player and move count. No persistent progress, scoring or undo yet.

## Verification
Recorded solution: from spawn (2,3), apply 90 degrees anticlockwise about (14,8), then hold right for 550 simulation ticks or until exit. Direct replay reaches the portal grounded with no respawns. An attempted walk and repeated jumps against the closed gate stop at its collision boundary. Tests cover intermediate-only arc collisions, thin obstacles, legal contact, signed long arcs, external centres, bounds extrema, player/hazard protection, exact geometry, inverse casts, reduced-motion and normal animation UI, reset and actual traversal. The prior translation tests must still pass. Actual browser appearance and touch ergonomics require separate review.
