## Level 36 — The Final Ascent
Two channels, a high hinged gate and a nine-unit summit with a star receiver. Five casts target three stars:
1. Translate the short deck by (20,-3), then cross to (39,3).
2. Reflect the deck in x=39 to the second channel, or rotate the independent raised span 90 degrees anticlockwise about (56,5.4).
3. Rotate the summit gate 90 degrees anticlockwise about (60,9).
4. Enlarge the ramp by factor 3 about (80,3), keeping its base aligned with the summit.
5. Reflect the summit crystal in x=45 to fit its receiver. Cross the second channel, walk beneath the gate and up the ramp to the portal.

Both recorded strategies use all four transformation types and five casts; both earn three stars. They differ in the second crossing's geometry and object use. The summit checkpoint is for recovery, not a scripted unlock.
