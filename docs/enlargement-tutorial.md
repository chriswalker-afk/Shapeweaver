## Mathematics and validation
P'=C+k(P-C) for positive k. Positive fractional factors shrink; factor 1 is a no-op; negative and zero factors are rejected. Type the factor (decimal or fraction) and both centre coordinates; the centre can be internal, on a vertex or external. The object may specify positive per-cast scale limits. Both endpoint geometry and complete motion are validated. In positive homothety, each vertex travels along a straight segment, so convex rectangular room bounds hold throughout when both endpoints are within them. For each convex triangle of a triangulated simple polygon, the union over factors between 1 and k is exactly the convex hull of its original and enlarged vertices. Check these swept hulls against triangles of every solid, hazard and player AABB with positive-overlap SAT. Decomposing before forming hulls preserves concave voids. This detects intermediate-only contacts with thin obstacles without fixed-time sampling. Boundary touching is allowed within numerical tolerance. Final support and player-supporting-object checks remain as in PR06.

The ghost labels corresponding points and rays through the chosen centre. Apply is enabled only for a full enlargement-path preview. Revalidate from current state and prevent applying edited parameters silently. The shared transaction updates immutable world geometry and collision index together; count only successful casts. Interpolate the positive factor from 1 to k over 0.8 seconds, pausing movement and competing spell inputs; reduced motion skips animation. Reset restores the original room and moves. The physics solver uses the enlarged triangle, not scaled artwork. The spell dispatcher is shared with translation and rotation; no changes to their mathematical rules.
