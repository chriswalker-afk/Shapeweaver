# PR10 — Reflection and The Missing Barrel

## Level 4
Select The Missing Barrel from the title. The room is 40 by 13 units, with floor y=3 and a raised ledge from x=28, top y=8. A sealed store on the left contains a 3-by-2.5-unit barrel at A=(3,3), B=(6,3), C=(6,5.5), D=(3,5.5). The apprentice starts at (10,3). The full-height wall and roof prevent normal access to the store. The intended reflection in x=15 places the barrel at x=24 to 27, y=3 to 5.5, so the apprentice can jump onto it and then onto the ledge. A'= (27,3), so the midpoint of A and A' has x=15. Three optional hints progress from the obstacle to midpoint and a worked equation. An altered orientation may require a different useful image; the last hint then labels the original worked example. The barrier is physical and the portal checks actual player position; no answer equality check. Other legal destinations and corrective reflections are allowed. The barrel uses a rectangular collision shape and reference-aligned timber/band/bung artwork.

## Mathematical and physical contract
Supported equations: x=a, y=b, y=x and y=-x, with finite signed decimal constants. Use explicit formulas from preview-math; no eval. Reflections preserve distances and area magnitude, reverse orientation, and are involutions. The image polygon is reversed back to CCW for the collision engine, while stable reference IDs are retained in parallel. IDs stay with the same material vertices after repeated reflections and other casts. The ghost labels corresponding points, draws the actual mirror line equation and perpendicular connectors with midpoint dots.

Reflection is deliberately a magical transfer: intervening barriers and hazards do not block it, unlike translation/rotation/enlargement. Destination must be in bounds, avoid positive-area overlap with terrain, solid objects, hazards and apprentice, and meet object support constraints. Moving an object supporting the apprentice is rejected. Legal boundary contact remains possible. The same explicit parameters are revalidated at Apply, then the immutable room and collision index swap atomically. Only successful casts count. The cast fades the source out then destination in, with a violet mirror shimmer; never model a squashed/intermediate physical object crossing a wall. The normal animation lasts 0.8 seconds; simulation and competing input are paused. Reduced-motion skips animation. Reset restores geometry, IDs, player and move count. Practice still only previews all four spells.
